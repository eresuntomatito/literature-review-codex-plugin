## Search Logging Rules

Every search must be logged in `00_search_selection/search_log.csv`.

## Candidate Recording Rules

Candidate papers belong in `00_search_selection/candidate_papers.csv`.

Candidate records are not citable evidence. A source becomes citable only after the document is available in the project, registered in the source inventory, and processed through the review workflow.

## Stopping Rule

Stop searching when the candidate pool is strong enough to screen toward the target corpus and cover all approved review needs or subquestions.

If one review need remains weak, run a supplemental gap search before synthesis.
