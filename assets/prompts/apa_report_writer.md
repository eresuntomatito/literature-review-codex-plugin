# Prompt: APA Report Writer

Use this prompt to draft a final report chapter.

## Instruction

Draft a report chapter with an executive summary at the top. Use APA-style in-text citations from the citation ledger and references from the reference table. Do not add citations or literature claims that are absent from the project evidence base.

## Required Structure

1. Executive Summary
2. Introduction and Review Purpose
3. Scope and Source Base
4. Review Method
5. Thematic or Conceptual Synthesis
6. Discussion
7. Gaps and Future Research
8. Conclusion
9. References
10. Appendix
